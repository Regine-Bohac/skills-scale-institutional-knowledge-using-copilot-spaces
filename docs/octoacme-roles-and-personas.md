# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
The Product Owner is accountable for prioritizing the work that delivers the most business value. They translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements and make trade-off decisions alongside the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize features and backlog items based on value and urgency
- Clarify requirements, acceptance criteria, and business intent
- Act as the main decision maker for scope trade-offs within the agreed delivery bounds
- Partner with the Project Manager and stakeholders to keep milestones realistic and aligned to business goals
- Review outcomes with stakeholders and support release readiness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with the Project Manager to align priorities, dependencies, and delivery commitments
- Collaborates with Developers and Product Managers to refine scope and acceptance criteria
- Provides direction to the team while coordinating with the Program Sponsor on strategic alignment and funding decisions

### Goals
- Maintain a clear, valuable backlog
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and delivery expectations
- Improve the speed and quality of prioritization decisions

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement and prioritization sessions
- Stakeholder reviews and feature sign-off meetings
- Release and roadmap alignment discussions

---

## Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead drives execution across the team, ensuring work is sequenced, dependencies are resolved, and delivery health is visible to stakeholders. They help keep the project moving while supporting the Project Manager and team leads.

### Responsibilities
- Lead sprint or delivery cadence and remove blockers that slow progress
- Coordinate dependencies across engineering, product, and operations teams
- Monitor delivery health, forecast risk, and escalate issues early
- Support planning, readiness reviews, and handoffs between project phases
- Partner with the Project Manager to maintain timeline realism and status accuracy

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to maintain team focus and resolve delivery blockers
- Coordinates with the Product Owner and Project Manager to translate priorities into actionable execution plans
- Shares status and risk insights with the Program Sponsor and stakeholders to support timely decisions

### Goals
- Improve predictability and delivery execution
- Reduce dependency bottlenecks and late-stage surprises
- Maintain a healthy and transparent operating rhythm

### Typical Communication
- Team standups, dependency reviews, and escalation calls
- Delivery dashboards and milestone readiness updates
- Cross-functional planning and issue triage sessions

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
The Change Manager ensures that project changes are understood, communicated, and adopted successfully. They help minimize disruption and support stakeholder readiness for new processes, systems, or updates.

### Responsibilities
- Assess the impact of changes across teams, users, and operating processes
- Develop communication plans and stakeholder engagement approaches
- Coordinate training, adoption support, and rollout readiness activities
- Partner with the Project Manager and Program Sponsor to manage risk and change fatigue
- Track user feedback and support transitions after launch

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to align communication timing with project milestones
- Coordinates with the Product Owner to ensure user needs and adoption concerns are reflected in the change approach
- Supports Developers and operational teams by clarifying adoption expectations and support requirements

### Goals
- Increase adoption and minimize resistance to change
- Ensure stakeholders are informed and ready for transitions
- Reduce the operational risk of rollout and post-launch issues

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and stakeholder briefings
- Communication plans, readiness reviews, and adoption check-ins
- Feedback loops with support teams and affected users

---

## Program Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Program Sponsor provides strategic sponsorship, decision-making authority, and cross-functional alignment for major initiatives. They ensure the project supports business priorities and that leadership is engaged when risks or decisions need escalation.

### Responsibilities
- Sponsor the initiative and align it with strategic goals and funding priorities
- Resolve escalations related to scope, priority, budget, or cross-team trade-offs
- Support major milestone decisions and risk escalation paths
- Champion stakeholder alignment and executive communication
- Provide governance and accountability for program outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to review status, risks, and priorities at a program level
- Aligns with the Product Owner on value delivery and business outcomes
- Partners with the Delivery Lead and Change Manager to support execution and adoption readiness

### Goals
- Ensure strategic alignment and successful value realization
- Support timely decisions that reduce delivery friction
- Maintain confidence in the project’s benefits and risks

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and executive reviews
- Risk escalation discussions and funding decisions
- Strategic alignment briefings and outcome reviews

---

## Stakeholder Liaison / Business Representative

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison or Business Representative is the bridge between the project and the broader stakeholder community. They gather input, represent business concerns, and help translate stakeholder priorities into project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Represent stakeholder interests and manage feedback from affected groups
- Surface concerns, dependencies, and cross-functional impacts early
- Help define success criteria from a business perspective
- Support communications that explain project decisions and outcomes
- Coordinate with the Project Manager and Product Owner to maintain alignment on expectations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with the Product Owner to validate priorities and business impact
- Collaborates with the Project Manager to keep communication channels open and decisions transparent
- Provides feedback to the Delivery Lead and Change Manager to improve readiness and adoption planning

### Goals
- Improve stakeholder trust and engagement
- Ensure business concerns are visible and addressed
- Strengthen alignment between project execution and stakeholder expectations

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder interviews, reviews, and feedback sessions
- Status briefings and impact summaries
- Decision and escalation conversations with project leadership

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These roles are intended to complement the existing project management model by clarifying ownership, decision paths, and collaboration across teams.
